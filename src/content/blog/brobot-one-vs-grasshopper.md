# Brobot One vs Grasshopper: AI Answering vs Virtual Phone for Small Business

Grasshopper is a well-known virtual phone system for small businesses and solopreneurs. It offers a professional business number, call forwarding, voicemail, and basic features without a desk phone. But if you need more than a virtual number and voicemail (like AI answering and built-in CRM for lead capture), how does Grasshopper compare?

We've done the research. Here's an honest comparison to help you choose.

## Grasshopper: Deep Dive

Grasshopper positions itself as a simple, affordable way to get a business number without a landline. You get a virtual number, call forwarding to your cell, voicemail, and basic call management. It's popular with freelancers, solo consultants, and small teams who want to separate personal and business calls.

**What Grasshopper offers:**

- **Virtual business number** (local or toll-free)
- **Call forwarding** to your existing phone(s)
- **Voicemail** with transcription (on some plans)
- **Basic call features** (extensions, hunt groups, simultaneous ring)
- **Mobile and web app** for managing calls
- **Simple setup** and flat-rate pricing

**What Grasshopper lacks for lead-focused small businesses:**

- **No AI answering.** When you can't pick up, callers go to voicemail. Grasshopper improves voicemail with transcription, but it's still voicemail. **85% of callers won't call back** after hitting voicemail. Grasshopper doesn't change that. There's no AI that answers, qualifies, and logs leads when you're busy or closed.

- **No built-in CRM.** Calls and leads aren't tracked in a unified dashboard. You get voicemail transcripts and call logs, but no contact management, deal pipeline, or automated follow-up. You need a separate CRM and manual entry.

- **No 24/7 automated lead capture.** After hours and weekends, callers hit voicemail. There's no system to answer, qualify, or schedule callbacks when you're unavailable. You're losing the same **30–40% of calls** that happen outside business hours.

- **Limited SMS.** Grasshopper focuses on voice. SMS capabilities are limited compared to a platform built for lead capture and text follow-up.

**Pricing context:** Grasshopper plans range from roughly $26 to $80+/month depending on features and extensions. It's affordable for a virtual number. But for lead capture, you're still relying on voicemail, and paying for a system that doesn't answer when you can't.

**Best for:** Solopreneurs and very small teams who need a business number and call forwarding, and for whom voicemail is acceptable. If lead capture and 24/7 answering aren't priorities, Grasshopper works.

## Who Should Choose Which?

**Choose Grasshopper** if:

- You need a simple virtual business number and call forwarding
- You're a solopreneur or very small team with low call volume
- Voicemail is acceptable and lead capture isn't your primary concern
- You want minimal setup and flat-rate pricing for a basic setup

**Choose Brobot One** if:

- You need to capture every call and never send anyone to voicemail
- You want AI that qualifies leads and logs to CRM automatically
- You're in an industry where speed to lead and 24/7 availability matter
- You'd rather have phone + CRM in one platform than forward and hope you answer
