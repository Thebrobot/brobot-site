# Brobot One vs Nextiva: AI Answering, CRM, and Small Business Phone Compared

Nextiva is a popular VoIP provider for small and mid-sized businesses. They offer voice, video, team chat, and CRM in a unified platform. But if you're a lead-driven small business that needs AI answering and 24/7 call capture, how does Nextiva stack up?

We've done the research. Here's an honest comparison to help you choose.

## Nextiva: Deep Dive

Nextiva positions itself as an all-in-one business communications platform. Voice, video conferencing, team messaging, and CRM (Nextiva CRM) live in one ecosystem. They serve everything from solopreneurs to mid-market teams and have strong brand recognition.

**What Nextiva offers:**

- **Cloud VoIP** with unlimited domestic calling on many plans
- **Video conferencing** and team chat (Nextiva One)
- **Nextiva CRM** for contact management, deal tracking, and pipeline visibility
- **Call recording and analytics** (often on higher tiers)
- **Integrations** with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, and productivity tools
- **Mobile and desktop apps** for anywhere access

**What Nextiva lacks for lead-focused small businesses:**

- **No built-in AI answering.** When you can't pick up, callers go to voicemail. Nextiva offers voicemail-to-email and call queuing, but no AI that answers, qualifies, and logs leads when you're busy or closed. In 2026, 85% of voicemail callers never call back. Nextiva doesn't change that.

- **CRM is separate from the call flow.** Nextiva has a CRM, but call logging and lead capture still require manual steps or integration configuration. Calls don't automatically populate contact records with full context unless you've set up workflows. Brobot One bakes CRM into the phone so every call is a CRM event by design.

- **AI features are add-on.** Nextiva Intelligence (transcription, summarization, sentiment) is available on premium tiers. It's not standard, and it's focused on post-call analysis, not answering when you can't. There's no 24/7 AI receptionist.

- **Pricing scales with features.** More AI, more analytics, more integrations mean higher tiers. Small teams may pay for video and chat they don't heavily use. The per-user cost can add up.

**Pricing context:** Nextiva plans range from roughly $20 to $40+ per user per month depending on features. CRM, advanced analytics, and AI capabilities typically require higher tiers. Expect to evaluate add-ons for full lead-capture workflow.

**Best for:** Businesses that want voice, video, chat, and CRM in one vendor and are okay with voicemail as a fallback. If your main goal is unified communications and you have capacity to answer most calls, Nextiva works.
