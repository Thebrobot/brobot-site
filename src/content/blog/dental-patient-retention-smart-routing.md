# Patient Retention: How Dental Offices Use Smart Routing to Save 25% of New Patient Calls

**58% of [dental offices](/industries/dentist)** miss at least 1 in 4 calls. A new patient is worth **$1,000 to $2,000+** over their lifetime. When that call goes to voicemail or rings out, most never call back. Smart routing and AI answering change the equation. Dental offices that implement these tools report recovering up to **25% of new patient calls** that would otherwise be lost. Here's how it works.

## The Dental Call Problem

Dental practices run on appointments. New patients call during business hours. They also call during lunch, after hours, and when the front desk is on another line. Peak times (open, lunch, close) create predictable gaps. When no one answers, the caller leaves a message (maybe) or hangs up and tries the next practice. **85% never call back** after a missed call. For a practice missing 25% of calls, that's a significant revenue leak.

## What Smart Routing Does

Smart routing means every call gets to the right place, even when the front desk is busy or closed. Options include:

**AI answering first.** When you can't pick up, an AI greets the caller, asks if they're a new or existing patient, captures reason for visit and contact info, and either schedules (if integrated) or holds the lead for callback. No voicemail. No "please leave a message." The caller gets a real response.

**Immediate text-back.** Even when the AI hands off for a human callback, the caller gets a text within seconds: "Thanks for calling. We'll be in touch shortly." That text drives up to **93% recovery** of callers who would otherwise disappear. They know they were heard. They're less likely to call the next practice.

**Priority routing.** New patient calls can be routed differently than existing patient inquiries. Urgent requests (pain, emergency) can get flagged. The right person gets the right call at the right time.

## The 25% Recovery Effect

Practices that implement AI answering plus smart routing report recovering **20 to 25%** of new patient calls that would have been lost to voicemail or no-answer. The math is straightforward. If you were missing 25% of calls, and you now capture 20 to 25% of those, you've effectively stopped the bleed. For a practice adding 50 new patients per month, that could mean 10 to 12 additional patients. At $1,000 to $2,000 lifetime value each, the ROI is clear.
