# Peak Season Survival: Why HVAC Teams Miss 71% of Revenue

**71% of [HVAC](/industries/hvac) companies** miss calls during peak season. That's not a typo. When summer breakdowns and winter furnace failures hit, customers call multiple contractors. The first to answer often gets the job. The rest get voicemail, and most callers never call back. If your team is on jobs, in attics, or between appointments when the phone rings, you're losing revenue you'll never see again.

Here's what the 2026 data shows for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical contractors, and how to stop the leak.

## The Peak Season Call Crunch

HVAC, plumbing, and electrical work is seasonal by nature. Summer means AC failures. Winter means furnace emergencies. Spring and fall bring maintenance and upgrade cycles. When demand spikes, so does call volume. But your dispatch team doesn't scale overnight. You're still running the same number of trucks, the same office staff. The phone rings 3x as often. Something has to give.

**85% of callers never call back** if their first call goes unanswered. They move to the next contractor on their list. **62 to 67% contact a competitor** instead. In peak season, that competitor is one tap away. A homeowner with a broken AC in July or a frozen pipe in January isn't going to wait for a callback. They need help now.

## What 71% Miss Rate Costs You

Each missed call in HVAC and plumbing represents hundreds to thousands in lost revenue. A repair runs $200 to $2,000+. A full system replacement can be $5,000 to $15,000 or more. If you miss 10 calls per day during a 60-day peak season, that's 600 calls. At a 71% miss rate, you're losing over 400 of those. Even at a conservative $500 average job value, that's $200,000 in vanished pipeline per peak season.

The same math applies to plumbing (burst pipes, water heaters, drain issues) and electrical (panel upgrades, emergency repairs). Service trades live on inbound calls. Miss the call, miss the job.

The solution isn't more dispatchers. Hiring for peak season is expensive, and you can't staff 24/7 for emergencies. The fix is **AI phone answering** that captures every call, qualifies the lead, and gets a tech scheduled or a callback on the calendar. When you're on a roof or under a sink, the AI answers. When you're free, you follow up with full context.

**75% reduction in missed calls** is the average when AI answering is implemented. For HVAC teams starting at 71% miss rate, that can mean answering 50% or more of previously lost calls. **93% of missed calls can be recovered** when an automated text-back goes out within 1 minute. The caller knows you got their message. They're less likely to dial the next contractor. You get the callback.
