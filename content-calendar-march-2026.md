## Research Snapshot (2026)

**VoIP / Phone:**
- 62% of small/mid-sized businesses use VoIP; only 37.8% of inbound calls are answered by a live person
- Small businesses lose $126,000+/year from missed calls; 85% of callers never call back
- AI phone answering can reduce missed calls by 75%; 93% of missed calls recovered with text-back within 1 minute
- VoIP market hitting $415B by 2034; SMBs driving 65% of growth
- 71% of HVAC companies miss calls in peak season; 58% of dental practices miss 1 in 4 calls

**AI / Chatbots:**
- 68% of U.S. small businesses use AI; 91% of AI-using SMBs report revenue increases
- AI chatbots improve customer response times by 72%; appointment booking up 35–40%
- Small businesses save $500–$2,000/month and 20+ hours/month with AI
- 82% of very small firms believe AI isn't applicable to them (opportunity gap)
- Conversational AI in contact centers projected to reduce labor costs by $80B by 2026

**Local SEO / Google Business Profile:**
- 46% of Google searches seek local info; 87% of consumers use Google for local business
- Google connects customers to U.S. businesses 2 billion times/month
- 64% of companies verified on GBP; profiles with photos get 30–50% more views
- 75% of top-ranking businesses complete profile descriptions; primary category is #1 ranking factor
- Top businesses average 200+ reviews; 10+ photos often doubles engagement

**Reputation / Reviews:**
- 97% of consumers rely on reviews; 88% trust them as much as personal recommendations
- 73% only trust reviews from the last month; 57% won't consider businesses below 4 stars
- 53% expect responses to negative reviews within 24 hours to one week
- 74.5% of SMBs say reputation is critical, but 87% fail to respond to negative reviews within a week
- 62% concerned about AI-generated fake reviews; 46% suspicious of perfect 5-star ratings
